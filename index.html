About Me

The Engineer Behind the Simulations

I'm an Aerospace Engineering graduate from the Institute of Engineering, Pulchowk Campus (Tribhuvan University), with a deep fascination for computational fluid dynamics, fluid dynamics, and unmanned aerial vehicle design. I graduated top of my class with a cumulative percentage of 84.48%, having studied aerodynamics, flight dynamics, propulsion systems, avionics, control systems, CFD, and aircraft manufacturing and maintenance processes along the way.

With hands-on experience in CFD simulations, flow visualization experiments, and airline operations engineering, I bridge the gap between theoretical concepts and real-world aerospace applications. I'm passionate about using simulation tools like OpenFOAM and ANSYS Fluent to solve challenging problems in aerodynamics, heat transfer, and propulsion systems. Outside of engineering, I enjoy music, travel, chess, and football.
