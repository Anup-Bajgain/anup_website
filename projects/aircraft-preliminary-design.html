Aircraft Preliminary Design

Nimbus is a fixed-wing, autonomous electric aircraft designed for crop dusting — spraying farmland with more precision and lower running costs than fuel-powered dusters, aimed at the medium-sized farms current options don't serve well.

Objective

Design a fixed-wing, autonomous electric crop-duster meeting the following mission requirements:

  • Hopper capacity: 200 L
  • Endurance: 35 minutes
  • Minimum take-off distance: 300 m
  • Cruise velocity: 35 m/s
  • Working velocity: 30 m/s

Approach

  • Defined a multi-segment mission profile (climb–cruise–descent) and sized the aircraft using a power-loading vs. wing-loading matching plot and V-n flight envelope
  • Selected a high-wing layout: NACA 2412 wing airfoil, NACA 0012 horizontal-stabilizer airfoil, NACA 2412 vertical-stabilizer airfoil
  • Verified stability and handling via dynamic-mode analysis (spiral, short-period, phugoid, dutch roll), CG-travel/neutral-point analysis, stall/climb/descent characteristics, take-off/landing performance, and a Cooper–Harper handling test

Key Results

  • Final design: MTOW 576 kg, empty weight 293 kg, 83 kg Li-ion battery pack (265 Wh/kg)
  • Cruise speed 40 m/s (max 48 m/s) at a 500 m cruise altitude
  • Take-off distance 460 m, landing distance 390 m
  • 14 m swath width, work rate of Rs 5/Ha, 60 Ha total coverage
  • Rated 4 on the Modified Cooper–Harper scale — moderate pilot workload, with some drift in disturbances requiring pilot compensation
  • Concluded the aircraft handled reliably and adapted well, positioning Nimbus to fill the gap between large fuel-powered dusters and the shortage of medium-farm options
